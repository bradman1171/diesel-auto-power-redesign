# Diesel Auto Power — Website Redesign

A redesign concept focused on helping diesel owners find the right parts for their trucks.

The website brings vehicle selection, product discovery, and purchasing information into a consistent experience across desktop and mobile.

[Explore the website mockup](https://pixel-perfect-blueprint-539.lovable.app/)

## Shop by truck

Start with a vehicle platform, year, and engine to browse relevant parts:

- Dodge / Ram Cummins
- Chevrolet / GMC Duramax
- Ford Powerstroke
- Light-duty diesel applications

The selected vehicle stays available while browsing, with application information shown on product pages.

## Find parts faster

Browse by engine generation, product category, or manufacturer. Search by product name, SKU, or brand, then narrow results with filters and sorting.

The catalog includes turbochargers, injectors, fuel systems, engine components, exhaust parts, and other diesel upgrades and replacements.

## Understand the product before ordering

Product pages bring together:

- Product images, descriptions, and specifications.
- Manufacturer and SKU information.
- Listed vehicle applications and fitment guidance.
- Core-exchange options and deposit explanations.
- Related product recommendations.
- Access to specialist support.

## Designed for desktop and mobile

The redesign uses dark navy surfaces, blue action buttons, bold headings, and clear product layouts. Mobile navigation and shopping controls adapt to smaller screens rather than simply shrinking the desktop design.

The homepage provides direct paths to vehicle generations, product categories, featured items, DAP products, sales, brands, and services.

## Shopping experience

The prototype demonstrates vehicle selection, catalog browsing, product search, core-exchange choices, cart updates, and a sample checkout.

Service and information pages cover injector testing, customer support, and the Diesel Auto Power story.

## Project status

This is a website redesign prototype for review and development planning, not a live store.

It does not process payments or orders, manage customer accounts, or provide live inventory. Prices, fitment, shipping terms, and illustrative core charges require confirmation before production use.

Production commerce is intended to remain in Miva Merchant, with the redesign applied to the storefront experience.

## Technical foundation
